How to Use

Simple steps for the best natural results.

Shikakai Powder

Mix the powder with hot water and let it rest to form a paste. Apply it as a hair pack, leave it on for 10 minutes, and then rinse thoroughly with normal water. No additional shampoo is required.

Herbal Hair Oil

Massage gently into the scalp and hair. For the best results, use at least 3 times a week, though it is gentle and nourishing enough for daily application.

Dry Fruits Powder

A perfect daily energy booster. Adults can consume this 1 to 2 times daily. For children, a single serving once a day is recommended.
